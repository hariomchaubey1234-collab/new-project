Our Story

Building the tools that power the next generation of digital-first businesses.

Born from frustration, built for performance.

In 2021, our founders were frustrated by the cluttered, slow, and overly complex enterprise software they were forced to use every day. They realized that businesses were wasting countless hours just trying to manage their tools rather than actually doing the work.

Lumina was created with a singular focus: to design a workspace that is incredibly fast, intuitively organized, and beautifully crafted. We believe software should feel like a natural extension of your team's workflow, not a barrier to it.

Sarah Jenkins

CEO & Co-Founder
